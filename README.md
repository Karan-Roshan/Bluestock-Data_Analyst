## Project 1: Mutual Fund Analytics Platform

### Overview

Built a data analysis workflow for mutual fund datasets to examine
historical Net Asset Value (NAV), industry trends, investor activity,
and fund performance.

### Work Performed

-   Ingested mutual fund data from CSV files and historical NAV data
    from an API.
-   Cleaned and standardized fields, dates, and transaction records.
-   Designed a relational SQLite database and used SQL queries for
    analysis.
-   Performed EDA on NAV trends, assets under management (AUM), SIP
    activity, investor segments, and portfolio allocation.
-   Analyzed fund returns and risk indicators, including CAGR,
    volatility, Sharpe Ratio, Sortino Ratio, alpha, beta, and maximum
    drawdown, where supported by the available data.
-   Prepared visualizations and structured analytical outputs to compare
    fund performance.

### Key Findings and Analysis

The analysis was designed to reveal NAV and AUM trends, changes in SIP
activity, differences across fund categories, investor distribution
patterns, and comparative fund performance. Risk and return metrics
provide additional context for evaluating historical performance.
Specific numerical findings should be added from the final analysis
outputs.

## Project 2: Nifty 100 Financial Intelligence Platform

### Overview

Worked on a financial analytics platform designed to organize and
analyze company financial statements for 92 Nifty 100 companies, using
historical financial data and supporting datasets.

### Work Performed

-   Built data ingestion and ETL workflows for financial Excel datasets.
-   Cleaned and normalized company identifiers, financial-year labels,
    and data fields.
-   Designed a SQLite database with related company, profit and loss,
    balance sheet, cash flow, and supporting data tables.
-   Applied data-quality checks for missing values, duplicates, invalid
    values, and consistency across financial records.
-   Analyzed profitability, leverage, efficiency, growth, valuation, and
    cash-flow indicators.
-   Worked with financial KPIs such as profit margins, ROE, ROCE,
    debt-to-equity, interest coverage, and free cash flow.
-   Explored company, sector, and peer-group comparisons to support
    financial screening and reporting.

### Key Findings and Analysis

The platform supports comparisons of company fundamentals across
reporting periods and peer groups. Financial ratios can help identify
differences in profitability, leverage, operational efficiency, and cash
generation. Sector benchmarks and valuation indicators provide context
for comparing companies. Any company rankings or conclusions should be
based on validated outputs from the implemented analysis.
